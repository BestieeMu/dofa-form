Welcome to Our Individuals and 
Their Families,
Thank you for choosing DOFA Pathways. 
We know that entrusting the care of your loved one to 
someone else is one of the most courageous decisions a family 
can make. It requires faith, vulnerability, and hope and we honor that deeply.
DOFA Pathways were not created from theory or distance. It was born from experience from the sacred journey of 
birt
hing and caring for my own son with many abilities, and learning firsthand what it means to advocate, to fight, to 
love, and to believe in someone's limitless potential. Being chosen as his mother ignited the vision that became DOFA 
Pathways. Combined with
my many years as a Direct Support Professional, Registered Nurse, and Case Manager, this 
journey shaped
Our promise:
Your loved one will be met with
dignity, compassion, respect, and unwavering belief 
every single day.
Their choices will guide us.
Their preferences will shape us.
Their quality of life will inspire us
.
Our 
name
reflects our purpose:
Developing Opportunities For All
This is not just a name. It is our calling
We are committed to being present for you 24 hours a day, seven days a week
whenever you need our approved 
services. You are not just receiving a provider; you are joining a community built on empathy, advocacy, and hope.
This package was prepared to help you understand your services and what you can expect from us. Please take t
ime to 
review and sign. If you have any questions, you are always welcome to contact our team or reach out to me personally.
Thank you for allowing us to walk this journey with you.
It is an honor we do not take lightly.
Atinuke Fadipe, MSN, RN, CM/DN
Founder & CEO,
DOFA Pathways Corporation

Your Rights as a DOFA Pathways Participant
You have the right to:
Be treated with dignity, respect, and cultural sensitivity at all times.
Receive services without 
discrimination based on race, color, religion, national origin, sex, age, disability, or 
sexual orientation.
Privacy and confidentiality of your personal and health information.
Participate in the development, review, and revision of your Individuali
zed Service Plan (ISP).
Refuse any service or treatment and be informed of the consequences.
File a grievance or complaint without fear of retaliation.
Access your records and request corrections.
Have visitors and maintain personal 
relationships of your choice.
Manage your own finances unless a guardian or representative payee has been appointed.
Live in a safe, clean, and accessible home environment.
Communicate privately with family, friends, advocates, and outside parties
.
Request a change in your case manager or service provider when feasible.
Your Responsibilities as a Resident
You are responsible for:
Treating staff, housemates, and visitors with courtesy and respect.
Informing staff of any changes in your 
health, medications, or personal situation.
Following the house rules and schedule as outlined by DOFA Pathways.
Participating in your service plan reviews and ISP meetings.
Notify
staff before leaving the residence as required by your support plan.
Caring for your personal belongings and shared common areas.
Refraining from bringing prohibited substances or items into the residence.
Providing accurate information during 
the intake and ongoing services.
Grievance Procedure
If you have a concern or complaint, please:
1
Speak with your assigned Program Coordinator.
2
If unresolved, submit a written grievance to the Program Services Administrator.
3
If still unresolved, 
contact the Executive Director and the Director of Programs.
4
Contact the Maryland DDA if still unresolved after the above steps.
By signing below, I acknowledge that I have received, read (or had read to me), and understand my Rights and 
Responsibilities as a DOFA Pathways participant.
