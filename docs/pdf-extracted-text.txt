Welcome 
To Our Individuals and Their Families, 
 
Thank you for choosing DOFA Pathways. We know that entrusting the care of your loved one to 
someone else is one of the most courageous decisions a family can make. It requires faith, 
vulnerability, and hope and we honor that deeply. 
DOFA Pathways was not created from theory or distance. It was born from lived experience from the 
sacred journey of birthing and caring for my own son with many abilities and learning firsthand what it 
means to advocate, to fight, to love, and to believe in someone's limitless potential. Being chosen as 
his mother ignited the vision that became DOFA Pathways. 
Combined with my many years as a Direct Support Professional, and as a Registered Nurse, Case 
Manager, this journey shaped our promise: 
Your loved one will be met with dignity, compassion, respect, and unwavering belief — 
every single day. 
Their choices will guide us. 
Their preferences will shape us. 
Their quality of life will inspire us. 
Our name reflects our purpose: 
Developing  Opportunities  For  All 
This is not just a name. It is our calling. 
We are committed to being present for you 24 hours a day, seven days a week whenever you need our 
approved services. You are not just receiving a provider; you are joining a community built on empathy, 
advocacy, and hope. 
This intake package was prepared to help you understand your services and what you can expect from 
us. Please take time to review and sign. If you have any questions or concerns, you are always 
welcome to contact our team or reach out to me personally. 
Thank you for allowing us to walk this journey with you. 
It is an honor we do not take lightly. 
Best Wishes, 
 
Atinuke Fadipe, MSN, RN, CM/DN 
Founder & CEO, DOFA Pathways Corporation.

FORM 9 — Participant Rights & Responsibilities Acknowledgement 
 
Your Rights as a DOFA Pathways Participant 
You have the right to: 
■  Be treated with dignity, respect, and cultural sensitivity at all times. 
■  Receive services without discrimination based on race, color, religion, national origin, sex, age, disability, or 
sexual orientation. 
■  Privacy and confidentiality of your personal and health information. 
■  Participate in the development, review, and revision of your Individualized Service Plan (ISP). 
■  Refuse any service or treatment and be informed of consequences. 
■  File a grievance or complaint without fear of retaliation. 
■  Access your records and request corrections. 
■  Have visitors and maintain personal relationships of your choice. 
■  Manage your own finances unless a guardian or representative payee has been appointed. 
■  Live in a safe, clean, and accessible home environment. 
■  Communicate privately with family, friends, advocates, and outside parties. 
■  Request a change in your case manager or service provider when feasible. 
 
Your Responsibilities as a Resident 
You are responsible for: 
■  Treating staff, housemates, and visitors with courtesy and respect. 
■  Informing staff of any changes in your health, medications, or personal situation. 
■  Following the house rules and schedule as outlined by DOFA Pathways. 
■  Participating in your service plan reviews and ISP meetings. 
■  Notifying staff before leaving the residence as required by your support plan. 
■  Caring for your personal belongings and shared common areas. 
■  Refraining from bringing prohibited substances or items into the residence. 
■  Providing accurate information during the intake and ongoing services. 
 
Grievance Procedure 
If you have a concern or complaint, please: 
1.  Speak with your assigned Case Manager. 
2.  If unresolved, submit a written grievance to the Program Director. 
3.  If still unresolved, contact the Maryland DDA Office of Consumer Affairs. 
 
You may also contact the DOFA Pathways main office with any concerns.
